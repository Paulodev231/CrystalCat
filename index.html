Crystal Care

Turning growth into real help

Crystal Care is the health-focused social-impact arm of Crystal Cat. Its purpose is to help vulnerable people and families who struggle to afford essential healthcare — visiting hospitals, identifying genuine need, and providing assistance where resources and proper processes allow.

  • Helping patients who cannot afford hospital bills
  • Assisting with essential medicines & supplies
  • Supporting people needing urgent treatment
  • Helping vulnerable families face healthcare challenges
  • Working with hospitals to verify legitimate needs
  • Transparent reporting & governance

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Crystal Cat ($CCAT)?

A community-driven token on BNB Smart Chain built around a long-term vision of real-world utility — particularly real estate — and social impact through the Crystal Care health initiative.

Is the project safe and transparent?

Liquidity is represented as locked for 2 years and ownership is renounced as a key transparency principle. Always verify on-chain status and official announcements independently before relying on them.

What are the taxes and limits?

3% buy tax, 3% sell tax, and a maximum wallet size of 4% of supply to discourage whales and protect the community.

Where can I buy it?

On PancakeSwap using the official contract address. See the “How to buy” section above for a step-by-step guide.

Are the real-estate and charity features live now?

No. Real-estate utility and Crystal Care are proposed, exploratory concepts. They will only be presented as confirmed after formal agreements and official announcements.
